# Weekly visual review, 2026-11-02

Method: local production build, headless Chromium with software WebGL, 1280x800 and 390x800. Shots: preloader, scene at 8 s, after pointer move, after scroll, help window open, mobile, `/timeline`, `/credits`. Reference sites judged from `benchmarks/BRIEF.md` only. No source changed.

## What is on screen
Big step up from last week. A faceted gem preloader (about 1.5 s of dark void) hands off to a ring of five floating low-poly islands (grass, strata, crystals, mushrooms) around a mint icosahedron, over a dark navy sky gradient with drifting ice shards. Name, tagline and two pill buttons sit on top. Help opens a terminal-style window. Pointer parallax works but is subtle. No console errors seen.

## Scores (1 to 10, honest)
- Intro ritual: 5. The gem is charming and the wait is short, but there is no prompt, no "start" beat and no camera move into the world.
- Sense of a world: 4. Islands give scale and depth, but the sky is a near-black void with no clouds, horizon, sun or ground below. It reads as a showcase, not a place.
- Interaction delight: 3. Only gentle camera tilt. Islands and crystal do not react to hover or tap, nothing to toy with.
- UI restraint: 8. Two small pills, clean title, readable help window. Mobile title breaks at the hyphen but stays legible.
- Polish and performance: 6. Frame was stable under software GL and the first paint is not blocked. Mobile crops the two side islands at the edges and the sky is too dark for the colorful islands to pop.
- Low-poly consistency: 6. Islands are rich and on palette. The hero icosahedron is plain and mint-only, so it looks like a different, unfinished asset beside them. Particles are all one ice tone.

## Compliance
No logos, wordmarks, brand marks or copied assets found in the shots or in `src` and `public`. `/timeline` and `/credits` exist, but both are bare dark pages that feel detached from the world.

## Gap to the brief
Ritual (partly met), explore by doing (absent), one metaphor (floating islands, forming), playful rewards (absent), minimal UI (met), opt-in sound (met by omission), touch and recovery (untested beyond layout), credits page (met, plain). Biggest levers now: a lighter sky with depth, hover reactions, and replacing the placeholder crystal (already ticketed).

## Top problems
1. Sky is too dark and empty, islands float in a void.
2. No hover or tap response on islands.
3. Plain hero icosahedron clashes with the detailed islands.
4. Mobile crops the outer islands.
5. Sub-pages do not share the world's look.
